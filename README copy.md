☕ SOM*Coffee — Discover the Universe of Perfect Coffee

Welcome to SOM*Coffee, a beautifully crafted website designed for coffee lovers seeking the perfect brew and cozy vibes. This website represents a fictional but realistic coffee shop brand offering a seamless experience of browsing menus, making bookings, and getting inspired by the coffee culture.

🌐 Live Website Features

🏠 Home
A welcoming hero section with strong visuals and a motivational tagline.
Call-to-action buttons like “Order Now” and “Explore More”.
📖 About
Showcases SOM*Coffee's vision and story: a blend of sustainability, quality, and comfort.
Embedded promotional video to enhance brand feel.
🛠 Services
Highlighted coffee subscription offers and limited-time product discounts.
Buttons for subscribing and saving on quality deals.
📋 Menu
Beautifully designed product cards for coffee and desserts.
Each item includes an image, description, price, and "Order Now" button.
📍 Contact Us
Contact form for user inquiries.
Embedded Google Map showing the physical location (Mogadishu, Somalia).
📅 Book It Now
Booking form to reserve a coffee table by date, time, and number of people.
Operating hours section for easy reference.
🔗 Footer
Social media icons for connectivity.
Contact details for direct support.
🧰 Built With

HTML5 – Semantic structure and accessibility
CSS3 – Custom styling and layout (linked via index.css)
JavaScript – Interactive hamburger menu and responsive navigation

Clone or download this repository.
